## 24 Nov 2026 · Project site goes up

This site now holds the hardware brief, the wiring and software plan, the parts list, the parts research brief, the vessel model page and a live 3D view of the CAD model.

## 24 Nov 2026 · CAD version 6, feedback servos

The three hobby servos were replaced by Waveshare ST3215 bus servos, which report their true angle and share one chained cable. The servo driver board became a bus adapter, the servos got their own 12 V converter, and the marker actuator got a small 6 V converter. The parts list total rose by about $25 to roughly $2,155.

## 24 Nov 2026 · Butterfly confirms real-time streaming

In a meeting with the team, Butterfly confirmed that its SDK can stream imaging in real time to other devices. That removes what had been the largest open problem in the Butterfly version, which was getting the image into the computer. Butterfly probes also offer Doppler and biplane imaging.

## 24 Nov 2026 · CAD version 5, floating head and linear rail

The probe head now hangs on two guide rods and lifts freely if pushed, so it can never press harder than its own weight. The Z slide moved onto a linear rail to resist the twist from the overhanging head.

## 24 Nov 2026 · CAD version 4, second design audit

A dedicated second Y motor was added after finding that the donor printer's fourth motor cannot drive a belt. The load cell amplifier moved onto the head, and limit switches were added to the list.

## 23 Nov 2026 · CAD version 3, first motion sweep

Sweeping the model through its full range showed the yoke hitting the Z rail before yaw reached 90°, so the reach bracket grew from 70 to 116 mm. The marker solenoid was replaced by a linear actuator with enough travel.

## 23 Nov 2026 · First parts list

Every part was matched to a real supplier listing with a part number and price.

## Nov 2026 · Enclosure design

The open gantry became a three-walled enclosure with a slanted front panel for the touchscreen and phone, and the electronics moved to the outside of the back wall.

## Nov 2026 · Marker instead of needle

The design changed from inserting a needle to stamping an ink dot, which proves the system can find and hit the right point without piercing skin.
