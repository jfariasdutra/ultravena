## 26 Nov 2026 · Design version 7 after the team review

The design was reworked after the team's design review, and the estimated cost fell from about $2,155 to about $1,390. Every page, the 3D model and the downloads now show version 7.

- The touchscreen and the phone are replaced by one iPad mini that drops into a printed slot on the front panel. The team's own app runs on it, the Butterfly probe plugs into it, and it talks to the Jetson over Wi-Fi.
- The two depth cameras are replaced by two webcams that track printed markers on the arm and on the probe head, plus four small distance sensors around the probe face.
- The $103 marker actuator is replaced by a pen lifted by a micro servo.
- The wrist band is dropped in favour of a hand grip, a forearm cradle and pressure pads laid out in two columns of three.
- Each tilt joint gets a spring to take up gear slack, and a small sensor board on the head gathers the head's sensors onto one cable.
- The emergency stop is now a normally-closed button that holds a relay in, so a press or a broken wire both cut motor power. The earlier button was the wrong type.
